Our Story

A Heavy Fabrication House Since 1989

Fairtech Engineers was established in 1989 in Chinchwad, Pune, as a specialist in heavy steel fabrication. Over three decades, the company has grown from a single shed into a three-unit manufacturing operation spanning approximately 60,000 sq ft of covered area across Pune and Vadodara.

Our core expertise lies in MS (Mild Steel) and SS (Stainless Steel) fabrication — from heavy-gauge transformer tanks and boiler structures to industrial chimneys, ducting and process vessels — built to OEM drawings, tolerances and delivery schedules.

  • 3 manufacturing units, ~60,000 sq ft combined covered area
  • MS & SS fabrication for OEMs and EPC contractors
  • 20-year vendor relationship with Thermax
  • Sister company: Gear Control

Certification

TUV Austria Certified ISO 9001:2015

Fairtech Engineers is certified by TUV Austria to ISO 9001:2015 for the scope of Manufacture & Supply of Sheet Metal Fabricated Assemblies.

The certified quality management system governs every stage of our work — incoming material inspection, in-process weld and dimensional checks, and final inspection before dispatch — ensuring consistent, documented quality on every order.
