Products

Heavy Fabrication Product Lines

Eleven product families in MS and SS, fabricated to OEM drawings and delivered to leading manufacturers and EPC contractors across India.

Transformer Tanks

Heavy-gauge MS transformer tank fabrication built to OEM drawings and tolerances, supplied to leading transformer manufacturers and EPC contractors. Precision welding and dimensional accuracy ensure fit-for-purpose tanks ready for oil-filling and testing at the customer's end.

Air Pre-Heaters & Water Pre-Heaters

Custom-fabricated boiler heat-recovery assemblies engineered for efficient heat exchange in industrial boiler systems. Built in both MS and SS to match thermal and corrosion-resistance requirements across sectors.

Hoppers & Grate Structures

Heavy structural hoppers and grate assemblies for material handling and combustion systems in boilers and industrial plants, fabricated for high load-bearing strength and long service life under continuous operation.

Boiler Structures & Economizers

Structural steel fabrication for boiler support frames and economizer casings, built to precise engineering specifications that integrate directly into OEM boiler assemblies.

Bag Filters

Dust-collection bag filter housings and structures fabricated for industrial air pollution control systems, supporting compliance with emission norms across process industries.

Vacuum Vapour Plant Vessels

Large-format MS/SS vessel fabrication for vacuum vapour processing applications, built with precision plate work and reinforced internal structures for structural integrity under vacuum conditions.

Chimneys

Industrial chimneys and stacks fabricated in rolled steel sections, engineered for structural stability and long-term exposure to high-temperature flue gases.

Heavy Ducting

Large-diameter industrial ducting fabricated for flue gas, air handling, and process gas routing, built to withstand thermal and structural stresses in continuous industrial use.

Structural Fabrication for Infra Projects

Custom structural steel fabrication — beams, frames, and platforms — for infrastructure and industrial project sites, delivered to project engineering specifications and site-ready standards.

Forging Furnace Fabrication

Heavy fabricated casings and structural components for industrial forging furnaces, built to withstand high-temperature operating environments.

Sewage Treatment Plant Tanks

Fabricated steel tanks for sewage and wastewater treatment systems, built for structural durability and long-term reliability in continuous water-treatment operations.
