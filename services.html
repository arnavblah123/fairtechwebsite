Services

Value-Added Capabilities Wrapped Around Fabrication

Beyond fabrication itself, we cut, coat, insulate, pack and execute complete EPC scopes — so equipment leaves our shops finished and site-ready.

Laser Cutting Services

Precision laser cutting for sheet metal components, delivering clean edges and tight tolerances for parts that feed directly into downstream fabrication and assembly.

Plasma Cutting Services

High-capacity plasma cutting for heavy steel plate, enabling fast, accurate profile cutting for structural and vessel fabrication work.

EPC for Chimneys, Ducting & Piping

End-to-end engineering, procurement, and construction execution for industrial chimney, ducting, and piping systems — from design coordination through fabrication, delivery, and site installation.

Pressure Vessels for Oil & Gas

Fabricated pressure vessels engineered to the specific process and material requirements of oil & gas sector applications.

Pressure Vessels for Waste Water Companies

Pressure vessel fabrication tailored for water and wastewater treatment processes, built for durability under continuous industrial operating conditions.

Industrial Painting, Blasting & Insulation

Complete surface-finishing services including sandblasting for surface preparation, protective industrial painting/coating, and insulation application, ensuring fabricated equipment is corrosion-protected and ready for field deployment.

Export Packing

Export-grade wooden crating and packing services for fabricated equipment, ensuring safe transit for domestic and international shipments.
