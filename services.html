Services

Value-Added Capabilities Wrapped Around Fabrication

Beyond fabrication itself, we cut, coat, insulate, pack and execute complete EPC scopes — so equipment leaves our shops finished and site-ready.

Laser & Plasma Cutting

Precision laser cutting for sheet-metal components and high-capacity plasma cutting for heavy steel plate — clean edges and tight tolerances for parts that feed directly into downstream fabrication and assembly, plus fast, accurate profile cutting for structural and vessel work.

EPC for Chimneys, Ducting & Piping

End-to-end engineering, procurement, and construction execution for industrial chimney, ducting, and piping systems — from design coordination through fabrication, delivery, and site installation.

Painting & Blasting

Sandblasting for surface preparation and protective industrial painting/coating, ensuring fabricated equipment is corrosion-protected and ready for field deployment.

Insulation & Packing

Insulation application for fabricated equipment, plus export-grade wooden crating and packing that ensures safe transit for domestic and international shipments.
